Our story

From a hostel desk to 50,000+ happy walls

What began as hand-cut stickers sold to classmates has grown into a full print studio. Today we design everything in-house with independent Indian artists, print on premium vinyl and museum-grade paper, and ship across the country within 48 hours.

Every product that leaves our studio passes a simple test — would we stick it on our own laptop, hang it on our own wall? If not, it doesn't ship.
